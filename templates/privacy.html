Privacy notice

SmartFin does not require an account. It stores the financial inputs and calculated plan you submit, plus chat messages if you use optional AI. Your browser receives a signed session cookie containing a random plan token, so the plan remains available in that browser for up to seven days. Clearing the cookie removes access to the saved plan. Expired plans are deleted during later plan activity.

Why data is used

Inputs are used to calculate and display your plan. If you opt in to AI, its planning summary and chat context are sent to Groq for explanations. Market-related questions may also be sent to Tavily when search is enabled. The calculator works without AI consent.

Control your data

Use Export data or Delete data on the report. Deletion removes records in this application's database; it does not retract information already sent to optional external providers or remove operator backups. Recalculating replaces the prior plan in this browser.

Security

SmartFin uses signed, HTTP-only session cookies and CSRF protection. If you share a browser, another person using it may be able to open the current plan. A public deployment needs a persistent private database, HTTPS, an operator contact, and an applicable privacy policy.
